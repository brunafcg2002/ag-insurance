# Website legal documents

Updated 24 October 2026. The footer provides Privacy, Cookies, Terms of use, Legal notice, Complaints/disputes, and a direct link to the official Electronic Complaints Book. The first five open accessible native dialogs; the book opens the official platform in a new tab. Both footer layouts retain 44px targets and use the same plain text styling for every legal action, including the direct book link. Instagram and Facebook are ordinary external links; no social widgets or tracking scripts are embedded.

## Scope and review

The [FSM legal area](https://www.fsm.pt/privacy-policy) inspired the grouping of documents. The owner also supplied [Vitorinos’s legal notice](https://vitorinos.pt/informacao-legal/) as a sector reference; no Vitorinos registration numbers or representation/payment powers have been transferred to this business. The copy is original European Portuguese, tailored to an informational insurance mediation site with a local `mailto:` form, GitHub Pages, OpenStreetMap/Esri tiles, and no configured analytics or advertising. It does not reproduce FSM's B2B garment business, automatic email service, retention periods, provider contracts, liability exclusions, or exclusive court clauses.

Do not treat the existence of separate Terms, Disclaimer and Cookies documents as proof that each document title is legally mandatory. The legal duties concern the applicable information and actual business practices. These texts are drafts for company/legal review and retain explicit `[PlaceHolder]` fields rather than unverified facts.

## Fields requiring company confirmation

See [privacy notes](privacy.md) for the privacy-specific fields. Also confirm:

- Legal name, NIF, registered office and relevant commercial registration.
- Identity, category, registration number/date and authorised scope of the mediator in the ASF register. Confirm insurer relationships, representation powers, advice model, remuneration and required pre-contractual information; the notice is not a substitute for the full applicable disclosure.
- Complaints contact, approved management procedure, required information, acknowledgement/response deadlines, follow-up and access arrangements. Have the company check the current sector rules, including Articles 36–39 of ASF Regulation 13/2020-R, rather than inventing internal procedures or service promises.
- Active company registration and exact legal identity on the Electronic Complaints Book, and physical-book arrangements. A generic platform link does not register the company or confirm that it is searchable. The direct footer link remains usable without a business-specific URL.
- Competent alternative dispute resolution bodies and actual memberships, contacts, territorial/material competence and eligibility. The CIMPAS link is general information, not a claim of membership. Do not add the discontinued EU ODR platform.
- Storage/cookies, if any, attributable to external services in the actual production configuration. The cookie policy distinguishes observed site code from provider-dependent behaviour and does not invent a cookie inventory or claim that IP requests are cookies. If non-essential storage/tracking is introduced, assess and implement consent before loading it.

No blanket waiver of statutory responsibility, forced acceptance by browsing, or exclusive forum is added. No analytics, consent platform, automatic sending service, third-party social embed, or company registration has been activated by this change.
